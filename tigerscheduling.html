Tiger Scheduling

Project Conception

Tiger Scheduling was a project I built to introduce myself to application development. I wanted to understand the process behind creating iOS apps and the work involved in delivering a thoughtful product to users.

To begin, I focused on a common challenge: forgetfulness. Whether it is a work task or a daily errand, small responsibilities are easy to overlook, and this app was designed to help users stay organized and on track.

The User Interface

While addressing this problem, I also wanted the app to feel intuitive and easy to use. I designed a streamlined interface that allows users to quickly enter key details and set a reminder time without navigating through multiple screens.

The interface was inspired by my desire for simplicity and by the university that shaped my academic journey. Clemson University's purple and orange palette, paired with a friendly tiger illustration, create a polished and recognizable design that reflects the app's purpose.

A Simple App to Conquer the Day

Tiger Scheduling is intentionally simple, but it is designed to help users organize their week with timely reminders. In a busy schedule, important information can easily be lost, and this app provides a practical way to stay informed and prepared for both personal and professional responsibilities without the complexity of a more intricate UI.
