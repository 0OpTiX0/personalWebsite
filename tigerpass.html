CUTigerPass

Project Conception

CUTigerPass emerged from my experience working in Clemson University's athletic ticketing office and my passion for building software applications.

Over the course of four years, I gained a deeper understanding of the operations and daily practices of the ticketing division, which exposed me to the challenges our organization faced. One issue that stood out was the lack of a secure and reliable marketplace for students to buy and sell tickets. Leveraging my technical skills, I set out to create a solution to address this problem.

What it Accomplished

CUTigerPass is a student-focused marketplace designed to provide Clemson students with a secure, exclusive platform for buying and selling tickets. In the past, students often relied on fragmented third-party channels, which created security and trust concerns due to limited platform exclusivity. As the platform continues to mature, CUTigerPass will offer a reliable and safe environment for transactions and community engagement.

Beyond ticket resale, CUTigerPass will also serve as a centralized source of information for students interested in Clemson Athletics. The platform will help students stay informed about upcoming events, increase awareness of underrepresented athletic programs, and foster stronger engagement across the university community. By connecting students to timely information, CUTigerPass supports a more informed and connected athletic culture on campus.

Legacy

While it was a fun project that got to work on, this site is no longer active as the demand for this platform wasn't enough to get this project off the ground and justify production. It can still be found on my GitHub where it can viewed.
